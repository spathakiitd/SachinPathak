Dr. Sachin Pathak

Senior Associate Professor, PHYSICS

Ph.D. (IIT Delhi) · Post-doctoral Researcher (South Korea). Dedicated to exploring the frontiers of Spintronics, Nanomagnetism, and Material Engineering. Combining advanced research with passionate teaching.

Publications Contact Me CV

Research Expertise

Focusing on cutting-edge materials engineering for next-generation non-volatile memory, logic applications, and spintronic devices.

Spintronics & MRAM

Design and development of Nano-sized Magnetic Tunnel Junctions (MTJ) and Process-in Memory (PIM) architectures. Exploring domain wall motion using Spin-Orbit Torque (SOT).

Nanomagnetism & Thin Films

Interface engineered magnetic anisotropy in multilayers (e.g., Co/Pd, Ta/CoFeB/MgO). Investigation of sputtering kinetics and pseudomorphic growth at interfaces.

Ultrafast Spin Dynamics

Magneto-optical characterization of magnetic materials. Using transient pulse currents for fast and efficient Spin-Transfer Torque (STT) switching in MTJs.
